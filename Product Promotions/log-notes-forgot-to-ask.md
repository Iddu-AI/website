# Video Script: Log Notes — Forgot to Ask Scenario

**Format:** Problem story → Creator intro → App demo
**Feature:** Log Notes — voice-powered pre/post visit notes with AI extraction
**Target audience:** Patients who visit doctors regularly, busy parents managing family health

---

## STAGE 1 — On Camera (conversational, slow build)

> "You finally get your doctor's appointment. You've been waiting three weeks for it."

> "You're sitting in that exam room, the doctor walks in, and your mind just… goes blank. You answer their questions, they give you some information, and before you know it — the appointment is over."

> "You walk out, get in your car, start driving home. And then it hits you."

> "'I forgot to ask about the chest tightness.' 'I forgot to ask if this medication is safe with what I'm already taking.' 'I forgot to mention that symptom that's been bothering me for months.'"

> "You can't go back. The next appointment is weeks away. So you just… let it go."

> "And it happens every single time."

---

## STAGE 2 — Cut (new angle or zoom in, energy shifts)

> "I'm Agastya Munnangi. I'm a high school student. And I built a feature that makes sure you never walk out of that appointment with unasked questions again."

---

## STAGE 3 — Cut to screen recording

> "Let me show you how it works."

*(App demo begins)*

---

## Demo Narration Notes

- Open the Today tab, tap **Quick Actions** on an upcoming appointment card
- Tap **Log Notes** — microphone opens
- Say: *"Before your visit — just speak. Ask me about my blood pressure medication side effects, ask about the chest tightness I've had for two weeks, ask if I should adjust my diet."*
- Show AI extracting and structuring those into clean action items
- Then show the post-visit flow: *"After you leave — speak again. The doctor said follow up in six weeks, increase the dosage to 10mg, avoid ibuprofen. Done. It's all saved, structured, ready to reference anytime."*
- Tap back to the appointment — notes are attached to that visit permanently

---

## Production Notes

- The emotional beat is the driving-home moment — pause after "let it go" for 1-2 seconds
- Stage 2 hard cut — short, confident delivery
- Demo should show BOTH use cases: pre-visit (questions to ask) and post-visit (what happened)
- Emphasize the voice-first aspect — you don't type anything, you just talk
- The AI extraction is the wow moment — show raw speech turning into structured notes
