# Video Script: Smart Email Import — Appointment Chaos Scenario

**Format:** Problem story → Creator intro → App demo
**Feature:** Smart Email Import — forward any appointment confirmation email to your personal iddu address and it automatically parses and creates a formatted appointment
**Target audience:** Anyone who books doctor appointments via email confirmation — essentially everyone

---

## STAGE 1 — On Camera (conversational, slow build)

> "Three months ago, you called a specialist. Waited on hold. Got through. Booked an appointment. They sent you a confirmation email. You said 'got it' — and moved on with your life."

> "Fast forward to last week. You're trying to remember — do I actually have that appointment coming up? When is it? Did I even confirm it? Or did I miss a callback and it got cancelled?"

> "So you open your email and start searching. You type the doctor's name — nothing useful. You try the clinic name — you get newsletters and billing statements. You scroll back three months through hundreds of emails — promotions, receipts, shipping updates — looking for one confirmation email that may or may not exist."

> "Then you check your texts. You scroll and scroll. Maybe they sent a reminder. Maybe they didn't. You genuinely cannot tell."

> "So now you have to call. You dial the office. You wait on hold — twelve minutes. You finally get someone, and you ask: 'Hi, I just want to confirm — do I have an appointment with Dr. Peterson?' And they say — 'Yes, it's tomorrow at 10 AM.'"

> "Tomorrow. You had no idea. You almost missed it entirely."

> "And the worst part? You did everything right. You booked it. They confirmed it. The email existed — you just couldn't find it when it mattered."

---

## STAGE 2 — Cut (new angle or zoom in, energy shifts)

> "I'm Agastya Munnangi. I'm a high school student. And I built an app that turns any appointment confirmation email into a fully organized appointment — in seconds. All you do is forward it."

---

## STAGE 3 — Cut to screen recording

> "Let me show you how it works."

*(App demo begins)*

---

## Demo Narration Notes

- Show a realistic appointment confirmation email in the Mail app (clinic name, date, time, address)
- Forward it to the user's personal iddu address — e.g. *yourname@iddu.app*
- Say: *"The moment it lands, iddu reads it."*
- Cut to the app — the appointment appears automatically, fully formatted: clinic name, date, time, address, all pulled and structured from the email
- Say: *"No manual entry. No copy-pasting. No searching later."*
- Show the appointment sitting in the Today tab timeline alongside the rest of the family's schedule
- Say: *"It's just there — organized, with everything you need, exactly where you'd expect to find it."*

---

## Production Notes

- This script has the longest Stage 1 — the buildup earns the payoff. Do not cut it short.
- Pacing: slow and relatable through the search frustration. Speed up slightly at "So now you have to call."
- "Tomorrow. You had no idea." — full stop. One breath. Let it land before continuing.
- "You did everything right." — this line reframes the blame. The system failed the user, not the other way around. This builds the most empathy.
- Stage 2 energy: relief, not excitement. The viewer should feel "finally."
- Demo should be fast and frictionless — forward → app opens → appointment is already there. The contrast with Stage 1's chaos is the entire point.
- Consider showing the email search chaos on screen during Stage 1 (real scrolling through a messy inbox) — extremely relatable visual
