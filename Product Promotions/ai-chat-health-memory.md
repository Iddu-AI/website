# Video Script: AI Chat — Your Personal Health Memory

**Format:** Problem story → Creator intro → App demo
**Feature:** IDDU AI Chat — conversational access to full appointment history, visit notes, costs, facility info, procedure codes, and wellness records
**Target audience:** Anyone who has ever struggled to remember their own health history

---

## STAGE 1 — On Camera (conversational, slow build)

> "You're sitting in a new doctor's office, filling out the new patient intake form."

> "'Date of your last physical?' You pause. Was it last March? The year before? You write something down and hope it's close."

> "'Any procedures in the last 12 months?' You stare at the ceiling. You think so — but you can't remember exactly what it was called, or when, or where."

> "'Are you currently on any medications prescribed at a recent visit?' You think you remember. But you're not a hundred percent sure."

> "So you leave half the form blank. Or you write 'will follow up.' And the doctor has to work with incomplete information about your own health — because you simply couldn't remember."

> "And it's not just forms. Your insurance company calls about a procedure code from six months ago. You have no idea. You're trying to remember what the specialist told you at your last visit. You can't find the number for the clinic you went to a year ago. You don't know what you paid at that appointment."

> "Your entire health history lives across scattered emails, paper receipts, and your own fading memory."

---

## STAGE 2 — Cut (new angle or zoom in, energy shifts)

> "I'm Agastya Munnangi. I'm a high school student. And I built an AI that actually knows your health history — because it's been tracking it with you the whole time."

---

## STAGE 3 — Cut to screen recording

> "Let me show you how it works."

*(App demo begins)*

---

## Demo Narration Notes

- Open the **AI tab** — show the IDDU Agent chat interface
- Type or say: *"When was my last doctor's appointment?"* — AI responds with the exact date, facility, and description
- Then: *"What notes did I log after that visit?"* — AI surfaces the action items and voice notes from that appointment
- Then: *"How much did that visit cost?"* — AI pulls the cost/spending data
- Then: *"What's the phone number for Northside clinic?"* — instant answer
- Then: *"Does Sofia have any upcoming appointments this week?"* — AI answers across the whole family
- Say: *"Everything you've ever added to iddu — appointments, notes, costs, facility details, your family's schedule — IDDU can answer questions about all of it. In seconds. Just ask."*
- Show the "SMART ACTIONS" section briefly — AI can take actions, not just answer

---

## Production Notes

- Stage 1 pacing: slow and relatable — let each blank form moment land before moving to the next
- The insurance call line and "fading memory" line are the emotional peak — don't rush past them
- Stage 2 key word: **"tracking it with you the whole time"** — emphasize this. The AI isn't searching the internet, it knows YOUR data
- Demo should feel like a real conversation — type questions naturally, pause for responses
- Show at least 4 different question types to demonstrate the breadth: history, notes, cost, family
- End on the family question — it reinforces that this isn't just for one person, it covers the whole family
