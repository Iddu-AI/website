# Video Script: Insurance Card — Front Desk Scenario

**Format:** Problem story → Creator intro → App demo
**Feature:** Digital insurance card — instant access, shareable via email or text
**Target audience:** Patients, busy parents managing family health visits

---

## STAGE 1 — On Camera (conversational, slow build)

> "You're at the doctor's office. You've been waiting in line at the front desk. You finally get to the front, and the receptionist says — 'Can I see your insurance card?'"

> "And that's when the panic starts."

> "You're digging through your wallet. Loyalty cards, gift cards, old receipts. Not there. You switch to your purse — hand sanitizer, lip balm, a snack, your kids' stuff. You're holding up the entire line. People are staring."

> "You find an old card — but wait, is this the current one? Did the plan change last year? You're not sure. So you hand it over anyway and just hope for the best."

> "Meanwhile the receptionist is waiting. The line behind you is growing. And you're standing there, red in the face, for a card that you technically have — you just can't find it."

---

## STAGE 2 — Cut (new angle or zoom in, energy shifts)

> "I'm Agastya Munnangi. I'm a high school student. And I built an app where your insurance card is one tap away — and you can even send it to the front desk before you even walk in."

---

## STAGE 3 — Cut to screen recording

> "Let me show you how it works."

*(App demo begins)*

---

## Demo Narration Notes

- Open an appointment card → show the **insurance card thumbnail** already visible inline
- Tap it — full insurance card appears instantly, front and back
- Say: *"Uploaded once. Available on every appointment, forever."*
- Show the **Email** and **Text** buttons at the bottom of the detail sheet
- Say: *"You can send it directly to the clinic before you even leave the house — so by the time you walk in, they already have it."*
- Show the send confirmation — one tap, done

---

## Production Notes

- The "people are staring" line is the peak embarrassment moment — let it breathe
- Stage 2 hard cut — lead with "one tap away", that's the contrast
- Demo should move fast — the whole point is speed. Card appears in under 2 seconds.
- Highlight the send-ahead flow as the upgrade: not just showing the card, but sending it proactively
- If filming Stage 1, consider acting it out physically — the bag-digging moment is very visual
