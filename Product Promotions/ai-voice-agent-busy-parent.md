# Video Script: AI Voice Agent — Busy Parent Scenario

**Format:** Problem story → Creator intro → App demo
**Feature:** AI Voice Agent
**Target audience:** Busy parents managing family appointments

---

## STAGE 1 — On Camera (conversational, slow build)

> "You just finished a long day. You come home, have dinner with your family, sit down on the couch, put on Netflix — and you're finally starting to unwind."

> "Right before you fall asleep, you're scrolling through your personal email. And then you see it."

> "You have a doctor's appointment tomorrow at noon. And you can't make it."

> "You tell yourself — okay, I'll call the office first thing when they open. But then you check your calendar… and you have a meeting. At the exact same time the office opens."

> "Now you're lying in bed, stressed, knowing you might get hit with a no-show fee — for something completely out of your control."

> "Sound familiar?"

---

## STAGE 2 — Cut (new angle or zoom in, energy shifts)

> "I'm Agastya Munnangi. I'm a high school student. And I built an app that handles that entire situation for you — automatically."

---

## STAGE 3 — Cut to screen recording

> "Let me show you how it works."

*(App demo begins)*

---

## Production Notes

- Stage 2 should feel like a hard cut in energy — short sentences, confident delivery
- "Sound familiar?" is the bridge line — do not cut it
- Stage 3 screen recording starts immediately after "works" — no pause or filler
- Demo should show: appointment card → AI call in progress → result overlay
