# Full App Demo Script — iddu Product Demo

**Format:** Face cam + screen recording (split or PiP — face visible throughout)
**Length:** ~4–6 minutes
**Tone:** Natural, confident, conversational — like showing a friend something cool
**Note:** AI Voice Call feature is covered in a separate video. This script covers everything else.
**Account to use:** Marcus Webb (marcus.webb.iddu2@gmail.com) — Premium account with full family

- Log in as Marcus Webb on a real device (not simulator)

## Production Notes

**Pacing:**
- Scenes 1–2: slow and deliberate — let the UI breathe
- Scenes 3–5: medium pace — show, tap, narrate
- Scenes 6–7: confident and fast — these are the "wow" moments
- Scene 9: slow down again — let the final line land

**Face cam guidance:**
- Stay visible the entire time — PiP bottom-right corner minimum
- Look at the camera (not the phone) when you speak scene openers
- Look at the screen when demonstrating — natural, not staged
- No big smiles or performance — calm confidence throughout

**Screen recording tips:**
- Use a real device, not simulator
- Enable guided access so no accidental swipes
- Turn off auto-brightness — keep screen at 100%
- Record in portrait for phone, landscape face cam separate if possible

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't rush the appointment detail in Scene 3 — that's where the insurance card lives and it needs to land
- Don't skip the AI chat responses in Scene 7 — wait for the full response before moving to next question
- Don't mention "premium" or "subscription" on camera

**Cut order recommendation:**
Scene 1 → 2 → 3 → 7 → 4 → 6 → 5 → 8 → 9

*(Lead with the wow — put AI Chat earlier in the edit than in the recording order)*
