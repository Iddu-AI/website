# Video Script: Today / Tomorrow Chip — At-a-Glance Scenario

**Format:** Problem story → Creator intro → App demo
**Feature:** Today / Tomorrow chip — contextual upcoming appointment indicator on the Today tab
**Target audience:** Busy parents managing family appointments

---

## STAGE 1 — On Camera (conversational, slow build)

> "You're in the middle of your workday. Back-to-back meetings. You suddenly can't remember — does Lily have her pediatric appointment today or tomorrow?"

> "So you open your calendar app. You scroll. You search. You switch to a different app. You scroll again. By the time you find the answer, you've wasted three minutes and completely lost your focus."

> "And the answer? It was tomorrow. You stressed yourself out for nothing."

> "Sound familiar?"

---

## STAGE 2 — Cut (new angle or zoom in, energy shifts)

> "I'm Agastya Munnangi. I'm a high school student. And I built an app that tells you that in one second — the moment you open it."

---

## STAGE 3 — Cut to screen recording

> "Let me show you how it works."

*(App demo begins)*

---

## Demo Narration Notes

- Open the Today tab — point to the **"TOMORROW"** chip at the very top
- Say: *"Right here. The second you open the app — you know. Today or tomorrow. No scrolling."*
- Show the chip switching between **TODAY** and **TOMORROW** depending on appointment timing
- Show that it reflects the whole family — not just the owner's appointments
- Point to the stacked appointment cards below it as supporting context

---

## Production Notes

- Stage 2 hard cut — short, punchy delivery
- "Sound familiar?" is the bridge — keep it
- Demo opens directly on the Today tab, no navigation needed — the chip is immediately visible
- Contrast moment: show a cluttered generic calendar app first (2 seconds), then cut to iddu — instant answer
